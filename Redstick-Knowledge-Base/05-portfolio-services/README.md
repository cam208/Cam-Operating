# 05 — Portfolio Services

What Redstick does *after* the wire clears. Our differentiator is founder-first
support at the earliest stage: operational expertise beyond capital. A $100K or
$250K check does not buy a board seat or a large ownership stake — it buys a
relationship, and the value we add is measured in the operating problems we help
founders solve, not the dollars we deploy.

> Canonical facts live in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). Where anything here
> disagrees with that file, that file wins.

---

## The one-screen version

- **We compete on help, not price.** A small check from Redstick has to be worth
  more than a bigger check from a passive fund. The support model in this section is
  how we make that true. See [`02-founder-support.md`](./02-founder-support.md).
- **The edge is operator-specific.** Cam ran multi-unit food-service operations
  (Tim Hortons franchisee); Shane is a manufacturing and supply-chain engineer. We
  lean into unit economics, multi-unit/franchise ops, food supply chain, and GTM to
  food/restaurant/grocery buyers. We do not pretend to add value everywhere.
- **Onboarding is a fixed 30-day program.** Every new company runs the same
  first-30-days checklist so reporting, KPIs, and the relationship are set up the
  same way every time. See [`01-onboarding.md`](./01-onboarding.md).
- **We take information rights and usually a board *observer* seat, not a board
  seat.** That is consistent with our check size and the terms in the
  [deal-triage skill](../../skills/deal-triage/SKILL.md). See
  [`03-board-governance.md`](./03-board-governance.md).
- **Support intensity follows the power law.** Most of our hands-on time goes to the
  companies with breakout potential and the companies in crisis; steady performers
  get the standard cadence. This ties directly to the construction model in
  [`../04-portfolio-construction/`](../04-portfolio-construction/).

## The mutual deal, in one table

Portfolio services is a two-way commitment. We say plainly what founders get from us
and what we need from them. Detail lives in [`01-onboarding.md`](./01-onboarding.md).

| Redstick commits to | Redstick asks of the founder |
|---|---|
| A 30-day structured onboarding | A monthly written update by the 5th |
| On-demand operator help (Cam) + supply-chain help (Shane) | Honest reporting — bad news early, not late |
| Warm, relevant intros (customers, hires, co-investors) | Info rights honored; observer kept informed |
| Follow-on / fundraise-prep support for the breakouts | Reasonable notice on financing events and pro-rata |
| A signal on pro-rata and reserves at the next round | A materials pack ahead of any board/observer meeting |
