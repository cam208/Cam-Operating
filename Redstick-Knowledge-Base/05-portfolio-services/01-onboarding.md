# 05.01 — Post-Investment Onboarding

The first 30 days after the wire set the tone for the whole relationship. A founder
who has a clean reporting rhythm, a baseline set of KPIs, and two useful intros in
the first month treats Redstick as an operating partner. A founder we go quiet on
after closing treats us as a line on the cap table. This document is the fixed
program that makes the first outcome the default.

> Canonical facts live in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). Where anything here
> disagrees with that file, that file wins. Illustrative KPIs and examples below are
> **illustrative only** — no real portfolio names or metrics.

---

## Principles

- **Same program every time.** Every company runs the same 30-day checklist so
  reporting and KPIs are set up identically. Consistency is what makes the Portfolio
  Snapshot comparable across companies.
- **Reporting is a condition of the relationship, not a favor.** We are cheap to
  work with and generous with help; in exchange we expect a written monthly update.
- **Set the KPI baseline before we start helping.** We cannot show value-add if we
  never recorded where the company was on day one.
- **Front-load the intros.** The fastest trust is built by being useful early. One
  relevant intro in the first month beats ten in month six.

## What Redstick commits to vs. what it asks

The relationship only works if both sides hold up their end. This is stated to every
founder in Week 1.

| Redstick commits to | Redstick asks of the founder |
|---|---|
| Respond to a founder ask within **2 business days** | A written update by the **5th of each month** |
| On-demand operator help from Cam; supply-chain help from Shane | Report honestly — surface bad news early, not late |
| Warm, relevant intros where we actually know the person | Keep the KPI set stable so trends are comparable |
| Fundraise-prep and follow-on support for companies that earn it | Give **reasonable notice** on financing events and pro-rata windows |
| A clear, early signal on pro-rata / reserves at the next round | Honor information rights; keep the observer genuinely informed |
| Confidentiality and no shopping of the company's data | A materials pack ahead of any board/observer meeting |

We do **not** commit to: guaranteed follow-on (reserves are earned, not promised),
being available 24/7, or adding value in domains outside our operator edge. Honesty
about what we are *not* good at is part of the pitch.

## Reporting cadence setup

- **Frequency:** monthly, due by the 5th, covering the prior calendar month.
- **Format:** the written template below. Prose is fine; a dashboard export attached
  is welcome but does not replace the written narrative.
- **Channel:** email to `olli@redstickvc.com`; Olli logs it and flags anything that
  needs Cam. A founder who prefers a shared doc can use one — Olli still pulls it in.
- **Escalation:** if an update is missed, Olli sends one reminder. Two consecutive
  misses is a relationship signal, not just an admin miss — Cam reaches out directly.
- **Quarterly:** every third update should include a short "state of the company"
  paragraph — runway, hiring plan, next fundraise timing.

## Monthly founder-update template

Founders copy this into an email each month. Kept deliberately short — five minutes
to write, not an hour. *(Figures below are placeholders.)*

```
REDSTICK PORTFOLIO UPDATE — [Company] — [Month YYYY]

1. TL;DR (3 lines max)
   - The one thing that went right:
   - The one thing that went wrong:
   - The one thing I need help with:

2. KEY METRICS (vs. last month)
   | Metric              | This month | Last month | Notes            |
   |---------------------|-----------|-----------|------------------|
   | Revenue / ARR       | $__       | $__       | contracted/recog |
   | Cash in bank        | $__       | $__       |                  |
   | Net monthly burn    | $__       | $__       |                  |
   | Runway (months)     | __        | __        |                  |
   | Core KPI (pick one) | __        | __        | e.g. units live  |
   | Pipeline / LOIs     | __        | __        |                  |
   | Headcount           | __        | __        |                  |

3. PROGRESS
   - Product / tech:
   - Customers / GTM:
   - Ops / supply chain:

4. LOWLIGHTS & RISKS
   - What slipped, what's worrying me, what could go wrong:

5. ASKS OF REDSTICK
   - Specific intros wanted:
   - Operator / supply-chain problems to work on:
   - Fundraise / follow-on: [timing, status]

6. NEXT MONTH — top 3 priorities
   1.  2.  3.
```

The single most valuable field is **Section 5 (Asks)**. A founder who leaves it blank
every month is either not using us or not being honest — either way, Cam follows up.

## KPI baseline

Captured in Week 2–4 so we have a day-one reference point. Pick the metrics that
actually govern the business rather than vanity numbers. Illustrative examples by
vector *(illustrative — not real companies)*:

| Vector | Illustrative core KPI to baseline |
|---|---|
| Labour Productivity (Robotics) | Units deployed; uptime %; labor hours displaced per unit |
| Optimization (CV/Sensing) | Throughput graded/sorted; accuracy %; paid pilots live |
| Decision Automation (AI/Ops) | Seats/sites live; net revenue retention; forecast error reduction |
| Input Efficiency (Bio/Materials) | Yield lift %; cost per unit output; offtake volume contracted |

For every company, regardless of vector, also baseline: revenue/ARR (label
contracted vs. recognized), cash, burn, runway, and headcount.

## How the Portfolio Snapshot gets populated

The Portfolio Snapshot is the Notion record we keep for every active company,
updated as founder reports come in. Olli owns it.

1. **On close** (Week 1): Olli creates the record — company, close date, instrument,
   cap, check size, entry ownership (informational), vector, founder(s), location +
   currency, info-rights/observer status, support tier (set by Week 4).
2. **Baseline** (Week 4): Olli enters the day-one KPI baseline.
3. **Each month**: when the founder update lands, Olli updates the metrics row,
   flags any material change, and notes open asks. A missed update is logged as a
   flag, not left blank.
4. **On events**: financing rounds, markups/markdowns, key-hire changes, and crisis
   triggers are recorded as they happen and cross-referenced to the relevant
   [`../09-playbooks/`](../09-playbooks/) response.
5. **Quarterly**: Olli does a full sweep so the Snapshot feeds LP reporting in
   [`../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/`](../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/) cleanly.

Label every figure `USD` or `CAD` — fund reporting is USD, many companies report
CAD. Mark anything conversation-sourced `[to confirm]` and inferences `[Assumed]`,
consistent with the provenance rule in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md).
