# 06 — LP Relations & Fund Ops

How Redstick keeps the people whose money we invest informed, and how the fund
itself is measured, marked, and administered. A $5M Fund I lives or dies on deal
quality — but the ability to raise Fund II lives or dies on how we report Fund I.
LPs back the next fund based on the trust and the numbers we build here.

> Canonical facts live in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). Where anything here
> disagrees with that file, that file wins. Anything not fixed in `CLAUDE.md` —
> fee, carry, waterfall specifics — is marked `[confirm with LPA/GP]` and must be
> read off the Limited Partnership Agreement, not this document.

## The one-screen version

- **We report to LPs on a fixed cadence: quarterly letter, annual meeting, year-end
  investor newsletter.** No LP should ever be surprised by something they read
  elsewhere first. See [`01-lp-comms.md`](./01-lp-comms.md).
- **Fund I is evaluated on MOIC, not IRR.** The multiple is the number that matters
  for a concentrated, power-law seed fund on a long hold. TVPI, DPI, and RVPI are the
  interim proxies until exits arrive. See [`02-fund-reporting.md`](./02-fund-reporting.md).
- **We mark conservatively — cost until a priced round or material event.** Early
  seed marks are noise; we would rather under-promise and let realized outcomes speak.
  See the valuation-policy table in [`02-fund-reporting.md`](./02-fund-reporting.md).
- **We report in USD; many deals are CAD.** Every figure is labeled and normalized at
  a stated FX rate. Currency discipline is a reporting requirement, not a footnote.
- **Strategic LPs get strategic reporting.** FCC and CFIN care about policy outcomes —
  Canadian, female, and Indigenous founders, clean-tech, regional development — on top
  of the financial pack. We track those flags at triage so we can report them cleanly.
- **Anything touching fee, carry, or the waterfall is read off the LPA.** This section
  frames the mechanics; the LPA holds the binding numbers. See
  [`03-fund-administration.md`](./03-fund-administration.md).
