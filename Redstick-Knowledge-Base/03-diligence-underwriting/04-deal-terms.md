# Deal Terms

What Redstick asks for, what it will accept, and what it walks from. Fund I writes small
checks, so the terms philosophy is **founder-friendly but disciplined on the few things
that protect the position and the fund's optionality.** Deal Terms is the 5% lens — it
rarely makes a deal, but bad terms can break one.

> This is operating policy, not legal advice. Actual instruments are executed with fund
> counsel. See [`../07-governance-compliance/`](../07-governance-compliance/).

## Instruments we invest through

| Instrument | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **SAFE (post-money)** | Most pre-seed/seed | Simple, fast, founder-friendly; cap is the key term |
| **Convertible note** | When a founder prefers debt/there's a maturity/interest need | Watch maturity and interest; model conversion |
| **Priced round** | Later seed / Series A, or when a lead sets a priced round | Model option-pool refresh dilution before close |

For SAFEs/notes, the **valuation cap** is the term that drives the entire EV model
(`entry_own = check ÷ cap`). Discount matters only when it prices below the cap.

## Terms scorecard (the 5% lens)

| Term | What we want | Acceptable | Walk / flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Valuation cap** | Reasonable for stage/traction | Modestly rich if quality is high | > $25M at seed with no revenue/deployed product → red flag |
| **MFN clause** | Yes (upside optionality) | Neutral if absent | — |
| **Pro-rata rights** | Yes, through Series A/B | Through A | None → note reduced follow-on optionality |
| **Information rights** | Yes (standard reporting) | Yes | None → hard to monitor; flag |
| **Board observer** | Case-by-case (see below) | Observer on larger/riskier positions | Board *seat* demand on a $100–250K check → decline |
| **Discount** | Standard (if noted) | — | — |

**Scoring:** Terms score 4 = Redstick cap confirmed + MFN or pro-rata secured; score 2 =
founder's cap only, Redstick's cap still TBD. If Redstick's cap is TBD, the whole
recommendation defaults to **WATCH**.

## Ownership is informational — never a term we fight for

Fund I has **no target ownership**. We do not negotiate for an ownership floor and we
never pass on ownership/round-size grounds. Entry ownership is recorded for the file at
`check ÷ cap`, nothing more. (This is the single most important terms rule — see
[`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md).)

## MFN and pro-rata — the optionality we do value

- **MFN** — if a better-termed deal is signed before our SAFE converts, we get those
  terms. Free optionality; ask for it. Flag it as upside in the model.
- **Pro-rata** — the right (not obligation) to maintain our stake in future rounds. This
  is how a small fund concentrates into winners via reserves/SPVs
  ([`../04-portfolio-construction/`](../04-portfolio-construction/) and
  [`../09-playbooks/02-markups.md`](../09-playbooks/02-markups.md)). Worth securing;
  never accept a **mandatory** pro-rata that exceeds our reserve strategy.

## Terms that trigger red flags or a walk

- **Valuation > $25M at seed** with no revenue or deployed product → red flag (PASS).
- **Cap table > 30% to non-operating parties** → red flag (misaligned incentives).
- **Mandatory follow-on commitment** — closing conditioned on committing to a future
  round, or a forced pro-rata beyond Fund I reserves → red flag (PASS).
- **Prior SAFEs/notes stacked** — not a walk, but all EV dilution goes
  `[provisional — cap table required]` until the cap table is produced.

## Negotiation posture (Stage 7)

- Move fast and be clean — our brand is founder-first and disciplined, not a haggler on
  a small check.
- The two things worth pushing on: a **cap we'll actually invest at** (scenario b in the
  model) and **pro-rata + info + MFN**.
- Always record whether the recommendation would change between the **founder's cap** and
  **Redstick's proposed cap** — that delta is the negotiation.
- Papering, side letters, and signatures run through counsel; the conflicts policy in
  [`../07-governance-compliance/03-conflicts-policy.md`](../07-governance-compliance/03-conflicts-policy.md)
  governs any related-party or allocation question.
