# Founder Assessment

The **highest-weight lens (40%)** and the one hardest to fake with a good deck. At
pre-seed/seed there is little else to underwrite — the product will change, the market
will surprise you, but the founders are who you are actually backing.

## What we are assessing

| Dimension | What "strong" looks like | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain experience** | Real, verifiable time inside food/ag/robotics; scar tissue from the actual problem | Zero domain experience; tourist in the sector |
| **Problem articulation** | Explains the problem from the *customer's* seat, in the customer's language | Talks features/tech, can't state the customer's pain |
| **Team composition** | Covers the core risk (technical co-founder for deep tech; operator for GTM) | Missing the co-founder the company obviously needs |
| **Decision-making** | Evidence-driven, updates on new information, prioritizes ruthlessly | "Platform for everything," no focus |
| **Resilience** | History of pushing through hard things; realistic about risk | Only ever had tailwinds; brittle under pushback |
| **Commitment** | Full-time; meaningful personal capital/opportunity cost in | Side project; hedging; no skin in the game |
| **Integrity** | Numbers reconcile; references confirm the story; no spin | Any misrepresentation → automatic PASS |

## The operator advantage in founder DD

Cam and Shane have run and engineered food operations. Use it: probe the founder on the
grinding realities — margins, labor, perishability, buyer behavior, supply chain. A
founder who lights up on operational specifics and one who deflects to vision are easy
to tell apart when the person asking has actually done the work. This is Redstick's
single biggest diligence edge; lean on it hard on the Founder lens.

## Founder-call structure (Stage 2, 45–90 min)

1. **Story & motivation** (10 min) — why this problem, why them, why now.
2. **Problem & customer** (15 min) — make them prove they understand the buyer.
3. **Product & technology** (15 min) — what's real vs roadmap; demo if it exists.
4. **Traction & unit economics** (15 min) — the single best signal, cited; the numbers behind it.
5. **Team & commitment** (10 min) — who's full-time, self-funded amount, gaps.
6. **The raise** (10 min) — round, instrument, timing, lead status, milestones the money buys.
7. **Disconfirming questions** (10 min) — actively hunt for reasons not to invest.

Capture provenance for every fact: mark conversation-only data `[to confirm on call]`
and inferences `[Assumed]`. Nothing from a call is treated as verified until documents
or references confirm it.

## Reference calls (Stage 4) — the highest-density evidence

Evidence hierarchy: **founder's words < proven behavior < independent verification.**
References are where independent verification happens, and **back-channel references
outrank founder-provided ones.**

- **Target 3–5 calls.** Mix founder-provided and back-channel (former colleagues,
  customers, co-investors, people who worked *for* the founder).
- **Ask behavioral, not vibes:** "Tell me about a time X was wrong — how did they
  handle it?" "Would you work for/with them again? Why or why not?" "What's the thing
  they're not telling me?"
- **Customer references** double as market validation — did the pilot renew, would they
  pay, what would make them switch away.
- **Integrity check is non-negotiable.** Any credible signal of misrepresentation is a
  hard stop, not a discount.

## Scoring the Founder lens

| Score | Evidence bar |
|---|---|
| 0 | Red flag (no team, integrity issue, zero domain experience) |
| 1–2 | Domain experience claimed but unverified; gaps in team |
| 3 | Adequate — credible team, some verification, no red flags |
| 4 | Domain experience **plus** track record or reference support |
| 5 | Exceptional — proven operators the references rave about, obvious founder-market fit |

Remember the ADVANCE floor: a Founder score below 2.0 caps the whole deal at WATCH,
regardless of how good the market or tech looks.

## Common founder-DD failure modes to guard against

- **Halo effect** — a charismatic founder inflating every other lens. Score lenses
  independently.
- **Pattern-matching bias** — "reminds me of a winner" is not evidence. Disconfirm.
- **Founder-provided references only** — always get at least one true back-channel.
- **Confusing confidence for competence** — pressure-test with operational specifics.
