# 08 — Brand & Team

How Redstick presents itself to founders, LPs, and the agrifood ecosystem, and how a
$5M fund staffs the work behind that presentation without over-hiring. Brand is not
marketing decoration here — it is a sourcing and trust instrument. The clearer and
more disciplined the positioning, the better the inbound and the easier the LP
conversation.

> Canonical facts live in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). Where anything here
> disagrees with that file, that file wins.

---

## The one-screen version

- **The brand is the operator.** Cam Crowder is an ex–Tim Hortons franchisee and
  multi-unit operator. Redstick sells founder-first support from someone who has run
  the food business, not just modeled it. That credibility is the differentiator —
  protect it, do not dilute it.
- **The three pillars are the whole story:** thesis clarity, process discipline,
  founder-first operator support. Every asset — deck, site, newsletter, LinkedIn post
  — should ladder to one of the three. See [`01-positioning.md`](./01-positioning.md).
- **Content compounds sourcing and LP trust.** We publish a small amount of high-signal
  material on a repeatable cadence and repurpose it hard. Content feeds the funnel in
  [`../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/`](../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/). See
  [`02-thought-leadership.md`](./02-thought-leadership.md).
- **We stay lean by design.** Cam (GP), Shane (co-founder), and Olli (AI EA) run
  Fund I. Humans are added only on milestone triggers, not on ambition. See
  [`03-hiring-roadmap.md`](./03-hiring-roadmap.md).

## Rules for anything that leaves the building

1. **Every external asset ladders to one of the three pillars.** If it doesn't, cut it.
2. **The brand color is Redstick green `#1C3A2E`.** One primary color, used with
   discipline. See visual notes in [`01-positioning.md`](./01-positioning.md).
3. **No fabricated metrics, awards, or logos.** Illustrative examples are always
   framed as illustrative. Track record is stated only once it is real and citable.
4. **Fund facts come from `CLAUDE.md`.** $5M Fund I, $100K/$250K checks, pre-seed–
   Series A, North America — never drift these numbers in a public asset.
5. **Voice is disciplined operator, not hype.** Plain, specific, founder-respecting.
