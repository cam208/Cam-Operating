# 08.02 — Thought Leadership

Content is a sourcing and trust machine, not a vanity exercise. Done right, a small
amount of high-signal, disciplined content compounds two assets at once: better
inbound deal flow (founders self-select toward a fund that clearly understands their
space) and LP trust (LPs see the thesis in action between updates). This document is
the content strategy — pillars, channels, cadence, backlog, repurposing, and how it
all feeds the funnel.

> Canonical facts live in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). Fund facts in any
> published piece come from there.

---

## Principle: publish less, repurpose more

A $5M fund cannot out-publish anyone on volume, and shouldn't try. The strategy is:
one substantive artifact per cycle, then atomize it across channels. Quality and
consistency beat frequency. Every piece must ladder to a positioning pillar (thesis
clarity, process discipline, founder-first operator support) or it doesn't ship.

Lean but reliable. Consistency is the point — a missed cadence erodes the trust the
content is meant to build.

## 5. Repurposing workflow

One substantial artifact becomes many. This is how a lean team achieves presence
without volume.

```
1 monthly substantive piece (thesis take / playbook)
        │
        ├─► 3–5 LinkedIn posts (one idea each, over 2 weeks)
        ├─► 1 newsletter section (rolled into year-end piece)
        ├─► talking points for the next podcast/webinar
        ├─► a direct-email nurture note to relevant founders/LPs
        └─► an evergreen line for the Investor Guide (if it's a thesis point)

1 ecosystem appearance (event/webinar)
        │
        ├─► 1 recap post (what we saw, what it means)
        ├─► 1 quote/insight added to the backlog
        └─► follow-up intros logged to sourcing network (section 02)
```

**Rule of thumb:** nothing is created once. Every original piece has a repurposing plan
attached before it's written.

## 6. How content feeds the funnel

Thought leadership is the top of the sourcing machine described in
[`../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/`](../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/). The mechanics:

- **Attraction:** founders reading thesis takes and operator playbooks self-select —
  on-thesis founders recognize Redstick understands their space and reach out warm.
- **Qualification:** publishing the ICP and process (see
  [`01-positioning.md`](./01-positioning.md) and process-transparency posts) pre-filters
  inbound, so more of what arrives is on-thesis.
- **Attribution:** inbound that cites a specific post/newsletter is tagged with source
  `Content` in the Notion Deal Pipeline, so we can see which pieces actually source
  deals.
- **Nurture:** ecosystem appearances generate warm intros that feed the network
  strategy ([`../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/04-network-strategy.md`](../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/04-network-strategy.md)).
- **LP side:** the same content, aimed at LPs, keeps the thesis visible between formal
  updates — reinforcing the LP relations work in
  [`../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/`](../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/).

**Review metric:** quarterly, count inbound tagged `Content` in the pipeline and note
which pieces drove it. Optimize toward pieces that source on-thesis deals, not toward
vanity reach.
