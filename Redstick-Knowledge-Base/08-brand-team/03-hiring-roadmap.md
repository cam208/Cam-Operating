# 08.03 — Hiring Roadmap

How Redstick staffs a $5M fund without over-hiring. The default is lean: a small team
plus AI leverage, adding humans only when a specific milestone makes the next role
unavoidable. Management-fee economics on a $5M fund are unforgiving — every hire is
paid for out of a fee stream sized for a small vehicle — so headcount is a deliberate,
trigger-based decision, not an aspiration.

> Canonical facts live in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). Fund size ($5M),
> deployment (~1/3 as of mid-2026), and target portfolio (15–20) come from there.

---

## 1. The economics that govern hiring

- **A $5M fund generates a small management-fee budget.** That budget must cover fund
  admin, legal, tools, travel, and any salaries. There is no room for a full-time team
  of employees. This is a feature: it forces focus and leverage.
- **Fund II is where the team scales.** Fund I is run lean by Cam, Shane, and Olli.
  Most human hiring is sequenced to *follow* proof of Fund I performance and the
  raising of a larger Fund II — not to precede it.
- **Fractional and variable-cost first.** Where a role can be fractional, contracted,
  or automated, do that before hiring full-time.

## 3. What to leverage AI / Olli for vs. hire humans for

The lean strategy depends on drawing this line correctly. Automate the repeatable and
the structured; reserve humans for judgment, relationships, and accountability.

| Leverage AI / Olli | Hire / assign a human |
|---|---|
| First-pass deal triage (screen, score, flag) | Final investment judgment and IC decisions |
| Inbox + calendar coordination | Founder and LP relationships |
| Portfolio-report intake and formatting | Board seats and governance accountability |
| Content drafting, scheduling, repurposing | Brand voice and original operator POV |
| Data-room organization, doc extraction | Technical/reference diligence that needs a person |
| Pipeline hygiene, reminders, follow-ups | Fund financials sign-off (regulated) |
| Research and market stress-test searches | Negotiation and term-sheet decisions |

**Principle:** Olli extends the team's reach on structured work so humans are spent
only where a human is legally, relationally, or judgment-wise required.

## 4. Sequenced hiring triggers

Roles are added when a milestone makes them unavoidable — not on a calendar. The
sequence roughly tracks: *Fund I deploying → Fund I substantially deployed / marking up
→ raising Fund II → Fund II deploying.*

| # | Role | Type | Trigger (the thing that must be true first) | Rough timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fund admin / fund accounting** | Fractional / outsourced | At first close — regulated financials and LP reporting cannot be DIY | Fund I, at close (in place) |
| 2 | **Fractional CFO / controller** | Fractional | LP reporting complexity rises, or Fund II diligence requires it | Fund I mid-life → Fund II raise |
| 3 | **Analyst / Associate** | Full-time (Fund II fee) | Deal volume exceeds what Cam + Olli can process at quality, *and* Fund II fee budget supports it | Fund II close |
| 4 | **Scouts / Venture Partners** | Variable (carry/small budget) | Need deeper coverage in specific sub-sectors or geographies before a full hire is justified | Fund I late → Fund II |
| 5 | **Platform / portfolio support** | Full-time or fractional | Portfolio large enough (approaching 15–20) that founder support needs a dedicated owner beyond Cam | Fund II deploying |
| 6 | **Second GP / partner** | Partner (carry) | Fund III scale / succession / check-size growth requires a second decision-maker | Fund II → Fund III |

> Order matters: variable-cost coverage (scouts, fractional roles) comes *before*
> full-time hires. The first full-time investment hire waits for Fund II economics.

## 6. Roles — what each actually does (when added)

- **Fund admin / fund accounting (fractional):** capital calls, distributions, K-1s,
  audit support, LP statements. Non-negotiable and regulated — outsourced from day one.
  Ties to [`../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/`](../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/).
- **Fractional CFO / controller:** fund-level financial oversight, valuations support,
  Fund II financial diligence. Fractional until scale justifies more.
- **Analyst / Associate:** sourcing support, first-draft diligence, model-building, and
  data-room work — under partner supervision. This is the first full-time investment
  hire; it waits for Fund II.
- **Scouts / Venture Partners:** part-time, incentive-aligned (carry or small budget)
  coverage of sub-sectors or regions the core team can't reach. Cheap breadth before
  expensive headcount. Feeds [`../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/`](../02-deal-sourcing-pipeline/).
- **Platform / portfolio support:** owns structured founder support and portfolio
  operations as the portfolio approaches 15–20 companies. Ties to
  [`../05-portfolio-services/`](../05-portfolio-services/).
- **Second GP / partner:** a Fund III-era decision, tied to check-size growth and
  succession — not a Fund I or early Fund II concern.

---

## 7. Lean-team operating principles

1. **Trigger, don't aspire.** A role is added when a named milestone forces it, never
   because peers have the role.
2. **Automate before you hire.** If Olli or a tool can carry structured work at
   quality, that comes first. Re-examine the AI-vs-human line (Section 3) before every
   hire.
3. **Fractional before full-time.** Variable-cost coverage (fractional CFO, scouts)
   precedes salaried headcount.
4. **Hire on Fund II economics, not Fund I.** The first full-time investment hire is a
   Fund II expense. Don't burden a $5M fee stream with a full-time team.
5. **Protect the founder-first promise.** As the portfolio grows, add support capacity
   before it strains — the operator-support pillar is a brand promise, not a nice-to-have.
6. **Keep decision-making concentrated until it can't be.** IC judgment stays with Cam
   (and Shane) until fund scale genuinely requires a second partner.
7. **Every hire has an owner and a written scope** before an offer goes out — no
   ambiguous roles on a team this small.

---

## Maintenance

Hiring-trigger review is **quarterly**, run against actual Fund I deployment and the
Fund II timeline (see [`../../operating-cadence.md`](../../operating-cadence.md)). If a
trigger condition is met, open the role; if not, the answer is "not yet" — and that is
the correct answer for a lean fund.
