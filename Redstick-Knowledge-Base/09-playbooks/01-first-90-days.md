# 09.01 — The First 90 Days

The post-close playbook: from the moment the wire lands to the first quarterly LP
update. The onboarding *program* (the fixed 30-day founder checklist) lives in
[`../05-portfolio-services/01-onboarding.md`](../05-portfolio-services/01-onboarding.md);
this playbook is the wider 90-day arc around it — including the internal work we do
*about* the investment, not just *with* the founder.

> Canonical facts live in [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). Checks are $100K or
> $250K only. We hold information rights and usually an observer seat, not a board
> seat. Everything below assumes influence, not control.

---

## The one-screen version

- **The first 90 days set the relationship and the data.** Founders remember the
  investor who was useful in week two, not the one who reappeared at the next raise.
- **We do two things in parallel:** (1) run the founder-facing onboarding program,
  and (2) write our own *memo of record* — the timestamped internal thesis we will
  grade ourselves against at 6 / 12 / 24 months.
- **KPI baseline before help.** We cannot tell if we added value, or if the company
  is drifting, without a clean starting line. Baseline first, then intervene.
- **Set the reporting habit early or never.** A monthly written update by the 5th is
  a habit built in month one. Companies that skip the first update rarely start.

## Week-by-week

### Week 0 — Wire & docs (the close is not the finish line)

| Item | Owner | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Funds wired; confirm receipt with founder | Cam | Founder confirms landing |
| Executed SAFE/note/priced docs countersigned and filed | Cam + fund admin | PDF in Drive deal folder |
| Cap table / SAFE ledger updated to reflect our entry | Olli | Reflected in cap-table doc |
| Side letter (info rights, pro-rata, observer) filed | Cam | Filed alongside main docs |
| Company added to Portfolio Snapshot (Notion) at **cost** | Olli | Row exists, marked at cost |
| Currency of the deal labeled (USD/CAD) on every field | Olli | All figures labeled |

> Mark at **cost** on entry. Per the valuation policy in
> [`../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/02-fund-reporting.md`](../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/02-fund-reporting.md),
> we hold cost until a priced round or material event — no day-one paper markup.

### Weeks 1–2 — Onboarding kickoff & the mutual deal

- Run the [30-day onboarding checklist](../05-portfolio-services/01-onboarding.md).
- Kickoff call: restate the two-way commitment (what we give / what we ask — see the
  mutual-deal table in
  [`../05-portfolio-services/README.md`](../05-portfolio-services/README.md)).
- Agree the **reporting contract**: monthly written update by the 5th; bad news early,
  not late; reasonable notice on financing events and pro-rata.
- Establish the observer relationship: meeting cadence, materials-pack expectation,
  how Cam wants to be reached between meetings.

### Weeks 2–4 — KPI baseline

- Capture the starting line for the metrics that actually govern this company's
  fund-returner path. Tie each to the vector it lives in (Robotics / CV-Sensing /
  AI-Ops / Bio-Materials).
- Typical baseline set: ARR (contracted vs recognized — state which), pipeline / LOIs,
  burn, runway (months), cash in bank, headcount, and the one or two operating KPIs
  unique to the model (e.g. units deployed, cost-per-pick, yield %, uptime).
- Store the baseline *inside* the memo of record so the 6/12/24-month revisit is a
  literal before/after.

### Weeks 3–6 — Network intros (earn the relationship)

- Deliver **1–3 warm, relevant intros** in the first six weeks — customers, a key
  hire, or a co-investor for the next round. Relevance over volume.
- Play to the edge: Cam for multi-unit / franchise / food-service GTM and unit
  economics; Shane for manufacturing, supply-chain, and production diligence. We do
  not pretend to add value everywhere (see
  [`../05-portfolio-services/02-founder-support.md`](../05-portfolio-services/02-founder-support.md)).

### Week 5 — First monthly update

- The first written update lands by the 5th of the month following close. Olli
  ingests it into the Portfolio Snapshot; Cam reads and responds within 48h.
- If it does not arrive: Olli nudges once, Cam follows up personally. The first miss
  is a relationship signal — treat it as one.

### Weeks 6–12 — First check-in & memo lock

- Substantive founder check-in (observer meeting or 1:1): are they on plan against the
  KPI baseline? What has changed since the memo?
- **Lock the memo of record** (template below) if not already done. This is the
  artifact the whole calibration culture depends on.
- Roll the company into the standard support cadence and the power-law
  support-intensity review.

### ~Day 90 — First quarterly LP update

- The company appears in its first quarterly LP letter — at cost unless a priced
  round or material event has occurred. Strategic-LP flags (FCC/CFIN: Canadian,
  female, Indigenous founders, clean-tech, regional development) are surfaced for the
  policy pack. See
  [`../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/01-lp-comms.md`](../06-lp-relations-fund-ops/01-lp-comms.md).

## Template — New Investment Memo of Record

An internal, timestamped document written once at close and **revisited (not
rewritten) at 6, 12, and 24 months**. Its purpose is calibration: to compare what we
believed on day one against what actually happened, and to teach the next underwrite.
Store it in the company's Drive folder and link it from the Snapshot row.

```
NEW INVESTMENT — MEMO OF RECORD                          [INTERNAL — CALIBRATION]

Company:            ____________________       Date of close: __________
Vector:             Robotics / CV-Sensing / AI-Ops / Bio-Materials
Instrument & terms: SAFE / note / priced — cap $__M [USD/CAD], discount __%,
                    MFN Y/N, pro-rata Y/N (through __ round), info rights Y/N,
                    observer Y/N
Check:              $100K / $250K (state which we actually wrote)
Entry ownership:    __% (informational only — never a gate)

WHY WE INVESTED (the thesis in 3–5 sentences)
  Founder signal:   ____________________
  Market / wedge:   ____________________
  Why now:          ____________________

THE FUND-RETURNER PATH (how this single deal could return $5M)
  Required exit ~$__M at ~$__M ARR × __x multiple, diluted retention ~__%
  (base dilution ×0.64; strong/fund-returner ×0.544). Name the path, not a hope.

THE BEAR CASE (≥20% fail probability — state it plainly)
  Most likely way this goes to zero: ____________________
  TVPI drag if it fails: 2% (@$100K) / 5% (@$250K)

KPI BASELINE AT CLOSE (the starting line)
  ARR (contracted/recognized): ____  Burn: ____  Runway (mo): ____
  Cash: ____  Headcount: ____  Vector-specific KPI(s): ____

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND (kill criteria / conviction triggers)
  We double down if:   ____________________
  We worry if:         ____________________

Strategic-LP flags: FCC / CFIN — Canadian / female / Indigenous / clean-tech / regional

---- REVISIT LOG (do not delete the original; append) ----
 6 mo  (date ____): held? drifted? surprised us? action: ____________________
12 mo  (date ____): ____________________
24 mo  (date ____): ____________________
```

> **Illustrative only:** a memo might project a fund-returner path of "~$221M exit at
> a $6M entry cap on a $250K check, at ~40% retained ownership." That figure is a
> worked example to calibrate against — not a forecast, and not a real portfolio
> outcome. The discipline is writing the number down so the 24-month revisit can grade
> it.

## Common failure modes (and the fix)

| Failure mode | Why it happens | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| No KPI baseline | Rushed onboarding | Baseline is checklist item #8 — no exceptions |
| First update never arrives | Habit not set in month one | Cam personally follows up on the first miss |
| Day-one paper markup | Optimism | Mark at cost; policy in 06 forbids early marks |
| Memo never written | "We know why we invested" | Memo is the calibration spine; lock by week 12 |
| Over-helping early | Eagerness to prove value | 1–3 *relevant* intros beats ten generic ones |

See [`../../operating-cadence.md`](../../operating-cadence.md) for the calibration
rhythm this playbook feeds.
